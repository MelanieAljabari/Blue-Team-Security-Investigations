## 🚨 Featured Investigation: Phishing Analysis 2

**Platform:** Blue Team Labs Online

**Investigation Type:** Phishing Email Analysis

### Investigation Overview

Analyzed a suspicious email impersonating a major online retailer.

The investigation involved examining email headers, identifying suspicious sender information, decoding encoded email content, and extracting potentially malicious URLs.

### Investigation Techniques

- Examined email headers and sender information.
- Identified potential brand impersonation.
- Analyzed MIME structure and Base64 encoding.
- Decoded HTML email content using CyberChef.
- Investigated suspicious URLs and redirects.
- Extracted external resources referenced in HTML.
- Identified indicators relevant to phishing investigations.

### Key Takeaways

This investigation reinforced the importance of analyzing email metadata, examining embedded links, and understanding how attackers use legitimate-looking branding to deceive recipients.

It also provided practical experience using cybersecurity investigation tools and documenting findings.

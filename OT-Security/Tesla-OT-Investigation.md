## Executive Summary

This investigation examined a simulated cybersecurity
incident involving industrial control systems at a
Tesla manufacturing facility.

The challenge focused on identifying unauthorized
activity within an operational technology network,
understanding industrial control communications,
and evaluating the potential impact of malicious
commands on manufacturing operations.

All five investigation questions were successfully
completed.

---

## Investigation Objectives

- Identify unauthorized OT network activity.
- Examine industrial communication functions.
- Understand Modbus holding register operations.
- Investigate suspicious register modifications.
- Analyze potential impacts on manufacturing equipment.
- Evaluate industrial safety and operational risks.

---

## Investigation Findings

### 1. Unauthorized OT Network Activity

Investigated the source of suspicious activity
within an industrial network segment.

The investigation emphasized identifying
unauthorized communications and understanding
their potential impact on industrial equipment.

### 2. Industrial Communication Functions

Examined Modbus function codes and their role
in industrial control system communications.

Understanding these functions helps analysts
differentiate routine monitoring from potentially
unauthorized control activity.

### 3. Holding Register Manipulation

Investigated an attempt to write an unexpected
value to a Modbus holding register.

Unauthorized register modifications can alter
equipment behavior and disrupt industrial processes.

### 4. Conveyor System Operations

Examined how industrial control commands can
affect conveyor operations.

Unexpected changes to equipment control values
may indicate malicious activity or operational faults.

### 5. SHORE_STOP_ACTIVE Signal

Investigated the operational meaning of the
SHORE_STOP_ACTIVE signal changing from 0 to 1.

The scenario highlighted how changes to
industrial control signals can affect equipment
availability and facility operations.

---

## Security Analysis

Industrial control systems require specialized
security monitoring because cyber incidents
can have physical consequences.

Potential risks include:

- Unauthorized equipment control
- Manufacturing downtime
- Process disruption
- Equipment damage
- Safety hazards

---

## Defensive Recommendations

1. Monitor Modbus write operations.
2. Restrict unauthorized access to OT networks.
3. Establish baselines for normal industrial traffic.
4. Alert on unexpected register modifications.
5. Segment IT and OT environments.
6. Maintain detailed industrial network logs.
7. Investigate abnormal equipment state changes.
